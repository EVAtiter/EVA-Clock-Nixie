# EVA Clock Nixie

**English** | [日本語](README.ja.md)

A nixie tube clock for your desktop.

The glowing orange digits are not photographs. Every tube outline and every bend
of every numeral is drawn from scratch. Each digit is built as a single bent
wire, and three layers — bloom, midtone, and glowing core — stack up to make
that particular orange light.

Digits that are not lit keep their glass envelope and the stack of cathodes
behind it. As the time changes, lit and unlit tubes quietly trade places.

It lives in the menu bar and keeps no icon in the Dock.

---

## What it does

- Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour display
- Resize from 25% to 150%
- Adjust transparency from 0% to 100% (still draggable when fully transparent)
- Float above other windows
- Lock the position to prevent accidental dragging
- Pin to the wallpaper, behind everything, on every Space
- Step aside automatically while another app is fullscreen, and come back when it exits
- Hourly chime, with a three-second countdown tone and adjustable volume
- Launch at login
- Remember its position per display, and return to it after sleep or a change of monitors

## Using it

Open the settings from the menu bar icon, or by right-clicking the clock itself.
Size and transparency have sliders you can drag while watching the result.

## Requirements

macOS 13.0 Ventura or later, Apple silicon.

## Privacy

This app collects nothing. No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising.
It performs no network communication at all — it runs inside the App Sandbox
without the network entitlement, so the capability is withheld by the operating
system rather than merely unused by the code.
